China KYC Questions, Answered

What is China KYC?

China KYC is identity verification for Chinese nationals using China-issued identity information and authoritative data sources. Financial institutions use it to verify Chinese individuals during customer onboarding.

How do Malaysian banks verify Chinese nationals?

With China KYC, banks verify name, ID card number and facial liveness, plus mobile number, bank card, criminal background, fraud and litigation checks in one real-time API flow.

What does a China KYB report include?

Company registration, shareholders, UBO, financial information, tax information, operation information, litigation records, judicial news, intellectual property and foreign trading information.
